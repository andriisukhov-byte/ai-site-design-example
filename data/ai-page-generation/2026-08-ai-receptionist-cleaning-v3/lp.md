## S19 - FAQ

Question 1: How is an AI receptionist for cleaning companies different from the answering service we already pay for?
Answer 1: An answering service takes a message, and your office manager still calls the customer back. The agent runs the lead from first ring to confirmed job, with live access to your booking software and calendar. It works on SMS, voice, web, email, and WhatsApp.
Question 2: What does the agent handle, and what does it hand to a human?
Answer 2: The agent answers calls and texts, qualifies the lead, quotes standard scopes, and books the crew. It also sends reminders, follows up on quotes, and offers the recurring plan. Your estimator handles commercial walk-throughs, and the agent escalates with the full history.
Question 3: How does it handle one-time deep cleans versus recurring customers?
Answer 3: One-time scopes get an instant flat rate and a same-week slot. Recurring customers pick a cadence, save a payment method, and get renewal reminders. The agent offers the recurring plan in the booking conversation.
